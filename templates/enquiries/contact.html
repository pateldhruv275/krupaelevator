Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Pre-Sales Questions

What is the typical lead time for elevator installation?

From final structural drawing approval to on-site testing and handover, custom passenger elevators generally require 6 to 8 weeks depending on floor height and cabin custom finishes.

Can Krupa elevators be installed in buildings with no pit or headroom?

Yes! Our MRL and hydraulic home elevator models require minimal pit depth (as low as 200 mm) and zero rooftop machine rooms, making them ideal for existing bungalows.

Does Krupa provide emergency passenger rescue mechanisms (ARD)?

Every Krupa passenger elevator includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) and emergency battery backup that brings the cabin safely to the nearest floor and unlocks doors during power failures.

What AMC plans are available for residential societies?

We offer both Comprehensive AMC (covering all spare parts, traction ropes, controllers) and Non-Comprehensive Preventive maintenance packages with 24/7 breakdown call response.
